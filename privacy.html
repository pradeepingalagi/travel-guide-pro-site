Privacy Policy for Travel Guide Pro

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 | Application: Travel Guide Pro

Summary: Travel Guide Pro is committed to protecting your privacy. We process translation inputs (audio, camera images, and text) ephemerally in real time to fulfill your translation requests, and when you choose to sign in with Google, we access only basic profile identifiers (openid, email, profile). We do not sell your personal data, share your recordings with advertisers, or access your Google Drive, Gmail, or Calendar.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Travel Guide Pro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, accesses, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use the Travel Guide Pro web application, progressive web app (PWA), Android app, and iOS app (collectively, the "Service").

2. Google Sign-In & Account Data

Travel Guide Pro offers optional authentication via Google Sign-In (powered by Firebase Authentication). If you choose to sign in with your Google account, we request only non-sensitive basic identity scopes:

  • openid & userinfo.profile: Your basic public profile details (display name and profile avatar URL) used strictly to display your signed-in identity inside the app.
  • userinfo.email: Your primary email address used strictly to identify your account, support account recovery, and verify account deletion requests.

We do not request any sensitive or restricted Google Workspace scopes (such as Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or Calendar), and we never transfer or sell your Google user data to third-party data brokers or ad networks.

3. Device Permissions & Functional Data We Process

To provide real-time travel translation and destination assistance, Travel Guide Pro requests device permissions solely when you actively use those features:

  • Microphone & Audio Data: When you tap the microphone to translate spoken conversation or voice queries, audio is recorded temporarily and sent over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to our server and Gemini AI endpoints solely to transcribe and translate speech. Spoken audio recordings are processed ephemerally and are never stored permanently or sold.
  • Camera & Image Data: When you use Camera Translate or Bill/Receipt Checker to read signs, menus, or receipts, captured image frames are transmitted securely for optical character recognition (OCR) and translation. Images are discarded after processing and are never saved to external public databases.
  • Location (GPS / Geolocation): With your explicit permission, your device location is used to fetch relevant local Travel Intelligence—such as nearest emergency hospitals, local emergency hotlines, local language detection, local currency, and current weather. Your GPS coordinates are not tracked continuously in the background and are never sold.
  • Local Storage & Preferences: Your recent translation history, saved phrases, and travel preferences are stored locally on your device so you can access them offline. You can clear local data anytime from Settings.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above strictly to:

  • Authenticate your optional Travel Guide Pro user session via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth.
  • Perform real-time voice, text, and camera translation and receipt checking.
  • Display localized travel guidance, emergency hospital info, weather forecasts, and currency conversion.
  • Protect service integrity, prevent abuse, and respond to user support or deletion requests.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Travel Guide Pro relies on trusted infrastructure providers under strict security and confidentiality obligations:

  • Google Cloud & Gemini AI: Used via secure server-side API calls for multimodal translation, speech transcription, OCR, and travel guidance.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google): Used for optional user sign-in and account lifecycle management.
  • Open-Meteo & Frankfurter API: Used for public weather forecasts and European Central Bank currency exchange rates (no personal user identity is sent to these endpoints).

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Voice audio and camera images submitted for translation are processed ephemerally in memory and discarded immediately once the translation is returned (0-day retention).

How to Delete Your Account & Data: You can permanently delete your Travel Guide Pro account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Open Travel Guide Pro, go to Settings → Account, and select "Delete Account & Erase All Data"; or
  2. Submit a request on our Account & Data Deletion Page or email pradeep.ingalagi@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Travel Guide Pro is a general travel utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Data Security

All data in transit between your device and our servers or third-party APIs is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS). We follow industry best practices to safeguard user data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

9. Changes to This Policy & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised Effective Date.
