Account & Data Deletion Request

Developer Contact: pradeep.ingalagi@gmail.com | Application: Travel Guide Pro

User Data Control:

Users have full control to delete their Travel Guide Pro account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to delete your data in-app or via email, what data is deleted, and the processing timeframe.

1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data using either method below:

Method A: Direct In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

Delete your account and all local data inside the app in 3 steps:

  1. Open the Travel Guide Pro app.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top header and open the Account tab.
  3. Tap "Delete Account & Erase All Data" and confirm.

Method B: Email Deletion Request

If you uninstalled the app or prefer email submission, contact our developer support email:

  • Recipient: pradeep.ingalagi@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Travel Guide Pro
  • Include: The Google email address used to sign in.
Submit Deletion Request via Email

2. Data Deleted vs. Retained

  • Authentication Record: Your Firebase Auth user record (User ID, display name, and email) is permanently removed.
  • Local History & Saved Phrases: All translation history, saved phrasebook entries, and preferences are erased.
  • Ephemeral Audio & Camera Inputs: Processed only in real-time memory and never retained (0-day retention).
  • Retained Data: No personal or identifiable data is retained after deletion is completed.

3. Processing Timeframe

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests to pradeep.ingalagi@gmail.com are verified and completed within 7 business days.
