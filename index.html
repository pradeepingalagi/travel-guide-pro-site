Travel Guide Pro

Travel Guide Pro is a cross-platform AI travel translator and destination companion available on Web, Android, and iOS. It helps international travelers communicate across languages in real time, read foreign signs and menus with their camera, check bills, and access essential local safety and travel intelligence anywhere in the world.

Purpose & Core Features of Travel Guide Pro

Real-Time Voice & Text Translation

Hold-to-speak and two-way live conversation translation across 100+ languages with natural text-to-speech playback for effortless communication with locals.

Camera & Receipt OCR Translation

Point your camera or upload photos of street signs, transit boards, restaurant menus, and receipts to translate text instantly and verify itemized bills.

Local Travel Intelligence

View destination-aware safety alerts, nearest emergency hospitals, local emergency phone numbers, tipping customs, and neighborhood travel insights.

Live Currency & Weather

Built-in live exchange rate converter powered by European Central Bank benchmark rates alongside real-time local weather conditions and forecasts.

Offline Phrasebook

Access categorized travel phrases for dining, transit, medical emergencies, and lodging—saved locally on your device even when mobile data is unavailable.

Cross-Platform Companion

Designed for seamless use on mobile web browsers (PWA), Android devices, and iOS devices with a clean, traveler-friendly interface.

How Travel Guide Pro Uses Google Sign-In & User Data

Optional Account Sign-In: Travel Guide Pro uses Firebase Authentication to allow users to optionally sign in with Google.

  • Requested Scopes Only: Only basic identity scopes (openid, email, and profile) are requested so the app can display your name, email address, and profile avatar in your account settings.
  • No Sensitive Google Workspace Access: Travel Guide Pro does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, or Contacts.
  • Ephemeral Translation Processing: Audio recordings and camera images used for translation are processed in real time and are not sold or used for third-party advertising.
  • Full User Control: You can erase your local history or permanently delete your account at any time inside the app or via our Account & Data Deletion page.
